Gathered, never picked

From the forestfloor to your home.

A Coco de Mer takes years to ripen on the palm. We only collect nuts once they have fallen naturally, under the watch of the Seychelles government. Each one is cleaned, dried, weighed and given its government seal before it can leave the islands.
