The tropics are all fun in the sun, except when it rains.

The rains in Seychelles can last upto a week due to the monsoon periods typical of the european “winter” season. So here is what you can do on a rainy day in Seychelles...

There is no need to fret! Take a bus or drive down to the south side of the island, 10 minutes past the airport you will reach a heritage site named Domaine de Val de Pres.

Behind it is the increasingly popular “ Coco De Mer Collection”

This is our family owned business which welcomes everyone Monday to Saturday from 9am to 4pm.

Here you will recieve an exclusive sneak peak behind one of the rarest industries in the world : The Coco de Mer processing.

The FREE tour includes tasting, a visit to both the kernel laboratory, a tour of the perfume laboratory and the final stop is our shop where you can try our selection of hand made fragrances.

Our welcoming staff will explain what we do and share the secrets behind the Coco de Mer and it’s all important conservation which we take to heart.
