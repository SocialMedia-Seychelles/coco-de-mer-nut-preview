In the world of luxury perfumes, there exists a realm of exclusivity where scent becomes an art form, crafted from the rarest and most exquisite ingredients. Enter the fragrant world of Lodas, Fanm, Zonm, Oud, and Sovaz – a collection of perfumes that embody sophistication, elegance, and the essence of the Seychelles islands.

Each bottle of fragrance from this exclusive line is a testament to opulence, meticulously formulated with the rare and coveted Coco de Mer nut, found only in the Seychelles archipelago. Renowned for its unique shape and its legendary reputation as an aphrodisiac, the Coco de Mer lends an unparalleled mystique to these perfumes, elevating them to a league of their own.

Let's delve into the olfactory journey of each fragrance:

  1. Lodas: With its intoxicating blend of citrus notes containing lemongrass, pink pepper and magnolia oil. Lodas captivates the senses with its allure and sophistication. Evoking the lush landscapes of the Seychelles. It's culminating in a scent that is both exotic and timeless.
  2. Fanm: Inspired by the strength and beauty of Seychellois women, Fanm exudes confidence and grace. Its aromatic profile combines delicate Jasmine with rich vanilla and Coco De Mer female, creating a fragrance that is as empowering as it is alluring.
  3. Zonm: A celebration of life and vitality, Zonm transports you to sun-kissed shores and swaying palm trees. Infused with notes of black pepper, cardamon and patchouly, this fragrance is a tropical paradise captured in a bottle, invoking feelings of joy and serenity with every spritz.
  4. Oud: For those with a taste for the exotic, Oud offers an enchanting journey into the heart of the Orient. This rich and complex fragrance blends the smoky, resinous notes of oud wood with hints of lavender and bergamot, creating a scent that is bold, mysterious, and utterly captivating.
  5. Sovaz: Inspired by the scent of the male Coco De Mer, Sovaz is a fresh and invigorating fragrance that evokes feelings of freedom and escape. Infused with notes of mint, grapefruit and white florals, this perfume is like a breath of fresh ocean air, rejuvenating the senses and lifting the spirit.
